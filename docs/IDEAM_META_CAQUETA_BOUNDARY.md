## Short summary

- **No source says which department layer IDEAM uses.** Neither the four semester reports nor the metadata of its hot spot portal. The hypothesis is neither confirmed nor contradicted by IDEAM.
- **It is documented, with sources, that two different lines between Meta and Caquetá have existed since September 2020:** the earlier official cartographic line and the provisional IGAC limit (the latter gives more territory to Caquetá).
- **It is documented that the DANE MGN versions use IGAC layers of different dates:** MGN 2018 = IGAC of May 2016 (before the provisional limit); MGN 2021-2022 = IGAC of June 2021 and November 2022; MGN 2023 = IGAC of May 2023 (both after it).
- **Direction of the error (an inference, not stated by any source):** the provisional limit gives Caquetá about 274,000 ha that used to belong to Meta. If IDEAM uses a layer later than 2020, it would have more points in Caquetá and fewer in Meta than an assignment with the 2016 layer. That matches the sign of our difference (ours minus IDEAM: Meta positive, Caquetá negative).

## 3. Dispute or ordinance about the limit

**There is a recognized and current boundary conflict.** Timeline, with all the sources even where they contradict each other:

1. **Legal basis.** The limit is described in the laws that created the departments: Law 118 of 1959 (Meta) and Law 78 of 1981 (Caquetá), referring to the source of the Ajajú river and the watershed between the Caquetá and Guayabero rivers. (Consonante, September 1, 2026; El Espectador, September 7, 2020.) The exact number of the Caquetá law is given by only one source: to be verified.
2. **IGAC technical report.** Presented to Congress (Territorial Planning Commission, COT) on August 21, 2020 according to El Espectador. According to the IGAC, Meta "owes Caquetá about 2,000 km2". The disputed zone is about 2,500 km2 with 112 villages (El Espectador). <https://infoamazonia.org/es/2020/09/07/la-pelea-entre-caqueta-y-meta-por-un-trozo-de-la-macarena/> (September 7, 2020; updated January 24, 2021)
3. **Senate, August 31, 2020 (official press release).** The territorial planning commissions decide "not to accept and, consequently, to return the technical report" of the IGAC for not complying with Law 1447 of 2011, to "maintain the limits ... as they appear on the current official maps" and to ask the IGAC to update the limits. <https://www.senado.gov.co/index.php/el-senado/noticias/1708-autoridades-y-comunidades-del-caqueta-piden-al-congreso-modificar-decision-del-diferendo-limitrofe-que-entrego-al-meta-3-700-kilometros-cuadrados>
4. **Contradiction with the previous source.** Guaviare Estéreo, September 15, 2020: according to the representative Harry González, "since this Monday, September 14" a provisional limit began to apply, which is the IGAC line; the note speaks of about 3,630 km2 in conflict. <https://guaviareestereo.com/se-aclara-area-limitrofe-a-favor-del-caqueta/>
5. **Status in 2026.** Lente Regional, May 21, 2026: the IGAC territorial director explained that "at present the limit between both departments is kept under the figure of a provisional limit" and that any change must be presented to Congress (Law 1447 of 2011). <https://lenteregional.com/en-san-vicente-abordaron-el-catastro-multiproposito-en-la-zona-limitrofe-entre-caqueta-y-meta/>
6. **Consonante, September 1, 2026 (journalism, not an official document).** It reports that: the IGAC created a tripartite commission in 2019 with the municipalities of La Macarena and San Vicente del Caguán; Caquetá has provisionally administered about **274,000 hectares since 2020**; the Council of State issued on March 6, 2024 an opinion that the IGAC made a methodological error when drawing the provisional line based on Decree 218 of 1973; Report No. 000016 of June 12, 2025 proposes keeping the official cartographic line in force before the provisional limit was implemented, so that Caquetá would lose those 274,000 ha; Congress has not resolved it. <https://consonante.org/noticia/el-mapa-de-la-discordia-el-catastro-rural-revivio-la-disputa-territorial-entre-meta-y-caqueta/>

**Not checked in the original documents:** the Council of State opinion, report 000016 of 2025 and the IGAC technical report (known only through the press). The contradiction between items 3 and 4 (Senate against Guaviare Estéreo) could not be resolved with official documents. What the two most recent sources (item 5, from the IGAC in 2026, and item 6, from Consonante) do say is that the provisional limit is the one in force today.

**Ordinance:** none found about this limit.

## 5. Other documented explanations

- **UTC date against local time.** The IDEAM portal metadata says FIRMS publishes in UTC and that its portal uses UTC-5, so "some night detections end up here on the day before the one FIRMS shows". It affects the semester cutoffs, but it does not explain a mirror effect between two neighboring departments.
- **NRT against the science archive.** FIRMS replaces the NRT data with science-quality data after a delay of about 5 months (FIRMS Archive Download page). The IDEAM portal uses NRT only (metadata, section 6). The semester reports do not say which one they use.
- **MGN version** (section 2): layers of different dates between versions.
- **Simplified national clip** of the IDEAM portal (metadata, section 6): it affects national borders and coasts, not the Meta - Caquetá border.

## Conclusion about the hypothesis

- **Confirms:** nothing says that IDEAM uses another version of the limits.
- **Contradicts:** nothing.
- **Supports indirectly (does not prove):** a provisional IGAC limit has been in force since September 2020 that moves about 274,000 ha from Meta to Caquetá (Consonante, 2026), and MGN 2018 uses an IGAC layer of May 2016, before that limit. The sign of our difference matches that change.
- **Assumption, not from any source:** that IDEAM uses a layer later than 2020.

## How to check it

1. Download the department layers of MGN 2018 and MGN 2023 from the DANE geoportal and compare the Meta and Caquetá polygons. If the area difference is close to 274,000 ha (2,740 km2) and lies in the La Macarena and San Vicente del Caguán zone, the 2023 layer includes the provisional limit. (The geoportal could not be downloaded automatically: it is a JavaScript app and the direct paths returned 403 and 404. Download it by hand.)
2. Reassign the detections with the 2023 layer and recompute Meta and Caquetá. If the gap with IDEAM closes, the hypothesis is supported by data. **`python scripts/compare_boundary_versions.py <2018 layer> <2023 layer>` does steps 1 and 2** once the 2023 department shapefile is unzipped (it needs a `DPTO_CCDGO` column).
3. To see IDEAM's own assignment: download from the portal the region CSVs for Meta and for Caquetá (2024-01-01 to 2024-06-30) and compare point by point with ours; the detections that IDEAM assigns differently mark its border. This only works for 2024, since the portal has no 2023 data.
